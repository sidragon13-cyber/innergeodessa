# InnerGeo Homepage Visual Design V1.0

Date: 2026-08-16
Status: Approved Design Baseline
Scope: Homepage visual system, information architecture, typography, brand presentation, and primary product entry structure.

---

## 1. Brand Positioning

Primary brand statement:

> Understand Yourself. Find Your Direction.
> 认识自己，找到更适合的方向。

InnerGeo is a focused self-discovery platform built around two clearly separated product paths:

### Structured Assessments
- Personality
- Career Interests
- Kids Interests

### Interest Exploration
- Zodiac Interests

The four products are all first-class product entries, but they do not share the same methodological positioning.

Personality, Career Interests, and Kids Interests form the structured assessment system.

Zodiac Interests is a formal self-exploration product and a strategic discovery / acquisition channel. It must remain clearly separate from the structured assessment category.

---

## 2. Product Architecture

### Structured Assessments

#### Personality
Purpose:
- Understand patterns in thinking, decision-making, and interaction.
- Provide deeper interpretation beyond a single personality label.

Frontend product name:
- EN: Personality
- ZH: 人格探索

Method terminology such as MBTI-style / 16 Types may appear in supporting professional descriptions, but should not replace the primary user-facing product name.

#### Career Interests
Purpose:
- Help users understand activities, work styles, and environments they are naturally drawn toward.
- Support career-direction exploration.

Frontend product name:
- EN: Career Interests
- ZH: 职业兴趣

RIASEC / Holland Codes are methodology terms, not the main frontend product name.

#### Kids Interests
Purpose:
- Help children and parents discover natural areas of curiosity and sustained interest.
- Maintain an age-appropriate, non-diagnostic experience.

Frontend product name:
- EN: Kids Interests
- ZH: 儿童兴趣

### Interest Exploration

#### Zodiac Interests
Purpose:
- Provide a lighter and engaging form of self-exploration.
- Act as a high-visibility acquisition and SEO entry point.
- Support natural cross-discovery into Personality and Career Interests.
- Preserve the existing Zodiac test, result, report, and location-based technical capabilities.

Frontend product name:
- EN: Zodiac Interests
- ZH: 星座兴趣探索

Boundary:
- Zodiac content is for interest exploration and entertainment reference.
- It is separate from InnerGeo's structured assessment system.
- It must not be presented as psychological diagnosis or professional assessment evidence.

---

## 3. Zodiac Strategic Role

Zodiac must NOT be reduced to a hidden or low-priority entertainment link.

Strategic role:

- Acquisition / discovery channel
- SEO traffic entry
- Shareable self-exploration experience
- Independent report product
- Cross-product entry into Personality and Career Interests

Recommended user journey:

Zodiac SEO / direct entry
→ Zodiac experience
→ Zodiac result
→ Zodiac report
→ "Explore yourself from a more structured perspective"
→ Personality
→ Career Interests

Kids Interests should not be forced into this cross-sell path unless relevant to the user context.

SEO direction:

- Evergreen Zodiac content
- Zodiac hub
- 12 sign pages
- Zodiac test
- Zodiac reports

Do not build the SEO strategy around:
- Daily horoscope content farms
- Tarot
- Fortune telling
- Future prediction
- BaZi
- Gambling / lucky-number content

Primary responsibility:

- Explain the InnerGeo brand.
- Establish trust.
- Communicate direction.
- Provide one clear primary action.

Recommended copy direction:

### EN
Understand Yourself.
Find Your Direction.

### ZH
认识自己，
找到更适合的方向。

Supporting message:

InnerGeo focuses on personality, career interests, and kids interest discovery through structured experiences designed to help users better understand their traits, interests, and possible directions.

Primary CTA:

- EN: Start Exploring
- ZH: 开始探索

- Start from any direction.

## 6. Hero Visual Language

Remove the existing constellation / seven-star / north-star visual identity.

Do not use a large astrology-like star composition as the main InnerGeo brand symbol.

Replace it with:

### Inner Map / Direction Map

Visual vocabulary:
- Paths
- Nodes
- Coordinates
- Contour-like lines
- Direction
- Soft geometric relationships
- One restrained gold destination / direction signal

Meaning:

You are here
→ Explore
→ Understand
→ Find direction

The Hero must visually communicate orientation and self-discovery rather than astrology.

Zodiac does not need to appear inside the Hero visual.

---

## 7. Product Universe

All four products must receive high homepage exposure.

They must appear in the first major product section after Hero.

### Structured Assessments

Three equal-weight cards:

- Personality
- Career Interests
- Kids Interests

They must share:
- Same color system
- Same card structure
- Same visual weight
- Same typography system

They must NOT be differentiated through purple / blue / orange product colors.

Differentiate through:
- Iconography
- Abstract visual language
- Copy
- Layout detail

### Interest Exploration

Zodiac Interests appears immediately after the three structured assessment cards within the same Product Universe.

It should use a wider dedicated product card / panel.

It should have strong visual presence but remain clearly labelled:

Interest Exploration

This produces:

Four high-exposure products
Two clear categories
One InnerGeo brand system

## 8. Trust / Method Section

Remove unsupported numerical trust claims including:

- 1,000+ organizations
- 100,000+ participants

Do not replace them with other unsupported large numbers.

Trust must be established through transparent product qualities.

Recommended trust pillars:

### Structured
结构化探索

Clear questions, dimensions, and result organization.

### In Depth
深入解释

Results should explain traits, interests, and direction rather than only output a label.

### Clear Boundaries
边界清晰

Used for self-exploration and development reference. Not medical or psychological diagnosis.

Recommended heading direction:

### EN
Built on thoughtful, structured methods.

### ZH
建立在成熟而清晰的方法之上。

## 21. Calm Assessment Experience

Assessment UI must feel like structured self-reflection, not an exam or game.

Avoid:

- Timers
- Correct / wrong feedback
- Red / green answer scoring
- Streaks
- Points
- Strong gamification
- Celebration overload
- Frequent popups
- Strong progress pressure
- Multiple saturated colors

Allow:

- Gentle progress indication
- Current question number
- Soft selected states
- Short transitions
- Large readable question text
- Generous whitespace
- Clear navigation

Core feeling:

"I am taking a few minutes to understand myself."

Not:

"I need to finish this test as fast as possible."

## 24. Strategic Design Rules

1. Professional does not mean cold.
2. Premium does not mean decorative.
3. Calm does not mean visually empty.
4. Zodiac is strategically important but methodologically separate.
5. Gold is a directional signal, not a dominant surface.
6. Product modules do not receive independent brand colors.
7. Unsupported trust numbers are prohibited.
8. Homepage visual language should communicate direction, not astrology.
9. Design must remain bilingual-friendly.
10. Every major element must improve trust, understanding, or next-step clarity.
