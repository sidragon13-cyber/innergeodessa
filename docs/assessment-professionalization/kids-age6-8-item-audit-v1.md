## 2. Items Requiring Revision

### K68-CREATE-06 — REVISE

**Issue:** Language and context are slightly complex for the youngest users and may depend unnecessarily on home/environment exposure.

**Preserve construct:** Spatial creative expression.

**Revision direction:** Use a simpler imaginary scene-making activity with ordinary materials and no assumption about access to a room that can be redesigned.

---

### K68-DISCOVER-05 — REVISE

**Issue:** The sequence involving two competing explanations, evidence search, and comparison creates excessive language and reasoning load for ages 6–8.

**Preserve construct:** Comparing explanations using clues.

**Revision direction:** Reduce to a simple two-possibility investigation.

---

### K68-DISCOVER-06 — REVISE

**Issue:** "Asking questions until you understand" may partially measure verbal behaviour, persistence, classroom norms, or confidence.

**Preserve construct:** Investigative curiosity.

**Revision direction:** Focus on wanting to find out how something works rather than the act of repeatedly asking questions.

---

### K68-THINK-04 — REVISE

**Issue:** Planning three moves in advance may partially measure planning preference rather than interest in structured reasoning.

**Preserve construct:** Strategic reasoning.

**Revision direction:** Focus on enjoyment of thinking about what might happen after several choices.

---

### K68-THINK-05 — REVISE

**Issue:** Wording is too linguistically complex for the lower end of the age band.

**Preserve construct:** Logical integration.

**Revision direction:** Use a simple visual clue-and-rule problem with minimal text.

---

### K68-CONNECT-03 — REVISE

**Issue:** Helping a child who does not understand creates moderate social-desirability pressure.

**Preserve construct:** Enjoyment of explaining/supporting learning.

**Revision direction:** Frame explaining as a neutral shared activity rather than a morally desirable rescue situation.

---

### K68-LEAD-02 — REVISE

**Issue:** "Project" and assigning parts resemble adult project-management language.

**Preserve construct:** Coordination.

**Revision direction:** Use a familiar shared game or activity.

---

### K68-LEAD-04 — REVISE

**Issue:** Helping everyone choose a plan may overlap with Connect through harmony/support behaviour.

**Preserve construct:** Decision coordination.

**Revision direction:** Emphasise moving several options toward one shared decision.

---

### K68-MOVE-02 — REVISE

**Issue:** Obstacle-course participation can depend strongly on mobility, physical confidence, space, and previous exposure.

**Preserve construct:** Enjoyment of active exploration.

**Revision direction:** Allow multiple forms of movement and avoid requiring athletic performance.

---

### K68-MOVE-06 — REVISE

**Issue:** "Using your body" can confound physical capability with activity preference.

**Preserve construct:** Embodied/active engagement.

**Revision direction:** Describe active participation without defining one physical ability as the required method.

## 3. Items Requiring Replacement

### K68-BUILD-05 — REPLACE

**Current construct:** Hands-on transformation.

**Problem:** "Use your hands" unnecessarily embeds a physical-function requirement and may disadvantage children with motor or physical disabilities.

**Replacement construct:** Direct manipulation and transformation of materials or objects using an accessible method.

---

### K68-CONNECT-05 — REPLACE

**Current construct:** Group support.

**Problem:** Helping a group understand what to do overlaps strongly with Lead/coordination and provides weak separation between Connect and Lead.

**Replacement construct:** Voluntary interpersonal support where another person's learning or participation is the central source of interest.

---

## 4. Connect Domain Warning

Connect has the highest social-desirability risk in the current eight-domain model.

Items must not effectively ask:

> Are you a kind child?

Future Connect items should increasingly use neutral preference comparisons or situations where multiple activities are equally acceptable.

The target construct is:

> Attraction to helping, teaching, explaining, cooperating, or supporting development.

It is not:

> Moral goodness.

---

## 5. Domain Separation Priorities

Priority boundaries for the next revision:

### Connect vs Lead

Connect:
support people and development.

Lead:
initiate, coordinate, influence, and move shared action forward.

### Discover vs Think

Discover:
investigate evidence and explanations.

Think:
solve rule-based or logical structures.

### Create vs Express

Create:
produce original form or content.

Express:
communicate meaning to others.

### Build vs Move

Build:
construct, repair, manipulate, or improve physical things.

Move:
active engagement with physical or natural environments.

---

## 6. Accessibility Priority

The highest accessibility risk currently exists in Move and selected Build items.

Interest must not be inferred from:

- running ability
- hand dexterity
- physical strength
- conventional mobility
- sports participation

Illustrations and wording must support multiple ways of participating.
