## 2. Meaning

### Required

Illustration support is considered necessary for comprehension,
construct clarity, accessibility, or reduction of language burden.

### Helpful

Illustration may improve comprehension, but its benefit must outweigh
the additional visual-bias risk.

### Text-Sufficient

The wording is sufficiently understandable without scored visual
support. Adding an illustration may introduce unnecessary bias.

---

## 3. Important Design Principle

The assessment does not aim to maximise the number of illustrations.

Illustrations are used only when they improve measurement quality.

Particular caution applies to:

- Connect
- Lead

because interpersonal illustrations can introduce social desirability,
status, dominance, emotional, or moral cues.

---

## 4. Change Control

Any future change to an item's illustration classification must trigger:

1. documented reason for the change;
2. visual-bias review;
3. accessibility review where relevant;
4. matrix version update;
5. regenerated SHA-256 fingerprint.
