## 1. Selection Principle

The Release Form selects four items from each of the eight interest domains.

Selection prioritises:

- construct clarity;
- low language burden;
- low domain overlap;
- low social-desirability contamination;
- low ability contamination;
- accessibility;
- low resource dependency;
- bilingual equivalence;
- context diversity.

Items not selected remain reviewed reserve items.
