## 1. Measurement Principle

Each item is designed to measure current attraction to an activity.

It does not directly measure:

- intelligence;
- school achievement;
- aptitude;
- fixed talent;
- professional ability;
- future occupation;
- future success.

For ages 9–12, items may include richer situations, strategy, persistence, independent exploration, and collaborative contexts, but interest remains the primary construct.
