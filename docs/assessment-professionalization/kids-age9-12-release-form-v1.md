## 1. Selection Principle

The Release Form selects five items from each of the eight interest domains.

Selection prioritises:

- construct clarity;
- domain differentiation;
- low redundancy;
- low social-desirability contamination;
- low ability contamination;
- age appropriateness;
- context diversity;
- accessibility;
- bilingual equivalence.

Items not selected remain reviewed reserve items.

# 11. Selected Item Order

The domain groups above define selection membership only.

They do not define the final assessment presentation order.

The final test must interleave domains so children do not answer five questions from the same interest domain consecutively.

Presentation-order design belongs to the scoring and assessment-form implementation stage.
