## 4. Domain Boundary Rules

### Discover vs Think

Discover:

Investigating why something happens through evidence and observation.

Think:

Enjoying rules, relationships, logical structures, and strategies.

### Connect vs Lead

Connect:

Supporting people's understanding, learning, or development.

Lead:

Initiating and coordinating shared action.

### Create vs Express

Create:

Generating original content, form, design, or ideas.

Express:

Communicating meaning to other people.

### Build vs Move

Build:

Constructing, modifying, assembling, or testing physical things.

Move:

Active exploration through interaction with space, environments, and activities.

## 5. Persistence Finding

Persistence items require special treatment.

Current persistence-format candidates are vulnerable to contamination from:

- ability;
- confidence;
- conscientiousness;
- frustration tolerance;
- previous success;
- adult expectations;
- task difficulty.

Persistence must not automatically contribute to the same core domain score as direct interest items.

Possible later treatment:

- rewrite as direct interest items; or
- retain selected items as separate Exploration Style / Engagement indicators.

This decision belongs to the scoring-design stage.
