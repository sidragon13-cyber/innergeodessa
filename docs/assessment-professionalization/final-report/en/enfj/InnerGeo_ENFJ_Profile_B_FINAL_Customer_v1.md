# 01｜Core Personality Profile

## A Direction Has Emerged, but Context Still Needs to Be Understood

Profile B shows a relatively stable ENFJ preference direction:

- external interaction may feel natural;
- long-term direction, patterns, and human development may attract attention;
- values, relationships, and human impact may enter judgment early;
- planning, commitment, and clear arrangements may feel natural.

At least one dimension is still moderately clear.

That means:

> **A direction has emerged, while behavior can still change noticeably across roles and environments.**

For example, you may move forward actively in familiar teams but observe first in unfamiliar settings. You may emphasize values in human issues and explicit standards in institutional issues. You may enjoy consensus while keeping more options open in innovative work. High interaction may work well, while complex judgment may still require independent time.

The Profile B task is to convert:

> “This is how I usually am”

into:

> **“When do I behave this way, and what results does it produce?”**

---

## Move from “What Does Everyone Need?” to “What Actually Works?”

ENFJ-related preferences may make it easy to notice:

- team emotion;
- relationship tension;
- who is being overlooked;
- who needs encouragement;
- who has not understood the goal.

Profile B should add another question:

> **Was my response actually effective?**

For example:

- Did encouragement improve actual performance?
- Did coordination resolve the real conflict?
- Did the meeting produce clear action?
- Did helping increase the other person’s autonomy?

This connects good intention with outcomes.

---

## Move from Consensus Orientation to Expressible Disagreement

A preference for values, relationships, and structure may make shared direction and relational clarity feel natural.

High-quality teams do not require continuous agreement.

Profile B can train:

> **Disagreement without Disconnection**

Useful practices include:

- allowing opposing views;
- understanding first, then deciding;
- separating the person from the viewpoint;
- not rushing to remove every uncomfortable feeling.

---

## Move from High Interaction to High-Quality Interaction

An Extraversion preference may make communication a rich source of information.

Profile B should distinguish:

> **Interaction Quantity**

from:

> **Interaction Quality**

High-quality interaction should:

- generate new information;
- clarify the problem;
- produce a next step;
- allow disagreement;
- avoid unnecessary attention costs.

# 02｜Thinking & Decision Structure

## Information Processing: Turn Human Signals into Verifiable Judgment

An Intuition preference may make you notice:

- potential relationships;
- long-term trends;
- hidden conflict;
- human development possibilities.

Profile B should practice:

> **Writing these judgments as testable hypotheses.**

For example:

> “The team may be losing motivation because the goal is unclear.”

Then ask:

- What evidence supports this?
- Has anyone said this directly?
- What other explanations exist?
- How can I test the interpretation?

---

## External Interaction: Build an Independent Framework Before Discussion

An Extraversion preference may make communication a natural way to form understanding.

For important issues, try:

### First Think
Form an initial judgment independently.

### Then Discuss
Discuss with others.

### Then Revise
Update the judgment.

This reduces overdependence on group feedback.

---

## Decision-Making: Values and Human Impact Need an Executable Structure

A Feeling preference may make you attentive to:

- fairness;
- respect;
- relationship impact;
- long-term values.

Real decisions still require:

- resources;
- rules;
- time;
- risk;
- responsibility;
- execution.

Use:

> **Values + Evidence + Boundaries + Action**

---

## Closure: Allow Some People to Disagree

An important Profile B exercise is:

> **Consensus is not always required.**

Some decisions only require:

- sufficient listening;
- clear standards;
- an explained decision;
- a review mechanism;
- continued respect.
