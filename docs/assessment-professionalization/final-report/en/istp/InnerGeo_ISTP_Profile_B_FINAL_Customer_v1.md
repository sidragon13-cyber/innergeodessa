---

## Move from Quick Fixes to Problem Classification

ISTP-related preferences may make it natural to:

- observe the symptom;
- find a likely key point;
- try a change;
- observe the result.

This works for many real-world problems.

Profile B can classify first:

### Routine Problem
Known issue; an existing method may be enough.

### Diagnostic Problem
A hypothesis needs testing.

### Novel Problem
Information is insufficient; more exploration is needed.

### High-Risk Problem
Risk and responsibility must be analyzed first.

Different problem types deserve different methods.

---

## Move from “Simple and Effective” to “Maintainable Over Time”

A short path can solve a problem quickly.

Long-term organizational systems also need to be:

- explainable;
- reproducible;
- maintainable;
- auditable;
- transferable.

Ask:

> **“Does this solution work only because I understand it, or can other people understand and maintain it too?”**

---

## Move from Autonomy to Clear Collaboration Interfaces

Introversion may make working independently natural, while Perceiving may make direct action and adjustment feel natural.

This can be efficient.

In multi-person systems, the issue is not “more communication.”

The issue is:

> **Clear interfaces.**

At minimum, other people should know:

- current status;
- risk;
- dependencies;
- decisions needed;
- next step.

# 02｜Thinking & Decision Structure

## Information Processing: Move from Real-World Feedback to Root-Cause Validation

A Sensing preference may make you sensitive to actual abnormalities and direct outcomes.

Profile B can use:

> **Symptom → Hypothesis → Test → Evidence → Root Cause**

Avoid assuming that one successful change proves you found the real cause.

---

## Internal Processing: Personal Experience Needs External Review

An Introversion preference may make independent judgment feel natural.

Complex problems often require:

- a second opinion;
- data;
- historical records;
- feedback from people involved.

For important judgments, use:

> **My View + External Check**

---

## Decision-Making: Logic and Efficiency Need Responsibility and Implementation Impact

A Thinking preference may make simple, fast, direct, and low-cost solutions attractive.

If a solution creates:

- difficult handover;
- user confusion;
- concentrated risk;
- poor maintainability for others;

it may not be the better long-term answer.

Use:

> **Efficiency + Reliability + Responsibility + Maintainability**

---

## Closure: Open Adjustment Needs Defined Review Points

A Perceiving preference may make continued adjustment feel natural.

Profile B can add:

- Review Date;
- Stable Version;
- Exit Criteria.

This reduces the risk of a system remaining permanently temporary.
