# 02｜Thinking & Decision Structure

## Information Processing: Move from Field Information to Key Variables

A Sensing preference may make it easy to notice:

- customer reactions;
- field conditions;
- resource changes;
- real constraints;
- current price or cost.

The Profile B development question is:

> **Which signals actually affect the long-term outcome?**

Ask:

1. Has this repeated?
2. Is it only an isolated case?
3. Which data can verify it?
4. What happens if it continues?
5. What is the worst-case outcome?

---

## External Interaction: High Interaction Does Not Mean High Information Quality

An Extraversion preference may make people a natural source of information.

But more conversation does not mean more accurate information.

Train yourself to distinguish:

- facts from opinions;
- commitments from intentions;
- interest from actual purchase;
- live consensus from long-term execution.

---

## Decision-Making: Short-Term Return and Long-Term Cost Should Enter Together

Profile B can also include:

> **Return + Risk + Reputation + Responsibility**

A Perceiving preference may make long-term lock-in feel uncomfortable.
