# 01｜Core Personality Profile

## Move from Rapid Action to Repeatable Execution Capability

Profile C shows a relatively clear ESTP preference structure.

You may often:

- obtain information quickly through external interaction;
- focus on real-world facts and current opportunities;
- use logic, cost, and causality in judgment;
- want to preserve room for adjustment;
- notice field changes and feedback quickly;
- prefer to validate direction through real action rather than prolonged discussion.

The most important boundary is:

> **Clear preferences do not mean that sales ability, decision-making ability, risk-management capability, or execution capability is already mature.**

Capability requires real-world evidence.

---

## Move from “Spotting Opportunities” to “Building an Opportunity System”

Spotting an opportunity may be the starting point.

Long-term capability also requires:

- opportunity sources;
- screening criteria;
- validation processes;
- risk controls;
- commitment management;
- outcome reviews.

Profile C can move from:

> **Opportunity Spotting**

toward:

> **Opportunity System**

---

## Move from Field Reaction to Repeatable Methods

Strong performance in a live situation may come from:

- experience;
- intuition;
- context;
- interpersonal judgment.

If the method can only be performed by you personally, it is difficult to compound.

Profile C can therefore ask:

> **Can this judgment be explained, recorded, and reused?**

Build assets such as:

- Playbook;
- Checklist;
- Sales / Negotiation Notes;
- Operating Rules;
- Risk Rules.

---

## Move from Flexible Action to Reliable Delivery

A clear Perceiving preference may make adjustment based on reality feel natural.

Long-term professional capability should also reach:

Build:

- Outcome;
- Owner;
- Deadline;
- Review Point.

A clear Sensing preference may make it easy to notice:

- current prices;
- customer reactions;
- resources;
- time;
- real conditions.

### Evidence

### Pattern

### Decision
A judgment formed from evidence.

## External Interaction: Move from Interaction Strength to Information-Quality Management

A clear Extraversion preference may make interpersonal interaction a stable source of information.

Profile C should train:

- identifying which sources are most reliable;
- distinguishing information from position-taking;
- confirming important commitments in writing;
- using data to check field judgments when needed.

---

## Decision-Making: Quantify Upside and Risk Together

A clear Thinking preference may make cost-benefit reasoning feel natural.

Profile C can use:

> **Expected Upside / Expected Downside / Reversibility**

Record at least:

- best case;
- worst case;
- likelihood;
- reversibility;
- maximum acceptable loss.

---

## Closure: Build Action Versions

A clear Perceiving preference may make continuous optimization feel natural.

Profile C can use:

> **Action Version 1.0 → Measure → Version 1.1**

This avoids restarting from zero every time something changes.

## A Core Profile C Risk: Mistaking an Immediate Win for Stable Capability

A single sale, a successful negotiation, or one quickly solved problem may all be influenced by:

- context;
- the counterparty;
- timing;
- available resources.

Real capability requires:

- repeated results;
- performance across different situations;
- consistency;
- explainable methods;
- review.
