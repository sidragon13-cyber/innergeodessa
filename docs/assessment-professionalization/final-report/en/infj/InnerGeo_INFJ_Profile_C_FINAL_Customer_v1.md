---

## Move from “I Understand” to “Is My Understanding Accurate?”

When deep interpretation forms naturally, the next developmental step is not:

> creating more interpretations.

It is:

> **Improving interpretation accuracy.**

A useful process is:

**Observation**

What did I actually observe?

↓

**Hypothesis**

How am I explaining it?

↓

**Direct Check**

How can I verify it?

↓

**Feedback**

How does the person or reality respond?

↓

**Revision**

What needs to change in my understanding?

This turns “I sense something” into more reliable understanding.

---

# 02｜Thinking & Decision Structure

## Information Processing: Move from Pattern Recognition to Evidence Quality

Clear Intuition may make it natural to notice:

- relationship patterns;
- potential direction;
- long-term themes;
- meaning and connection.

Profile C should train:

> **Which patterns deserve confidence?**

Ask:

1. What behavior supports this judgment?
2. What alternative explanations exist?
3. Has it been checked directly?
4. Is it based on only one event?
5. Does the pattern repeat across several contexts?

---

## Independent Processing: Move from Internal Understanding to External Visibility

Clear Introversion may mean much important thinking happens internally.

If other people cannot see the process, they may be unable to:

- understand your judgment;
- correct a misunderstanding;
- know what you need;
- participate in real collaboration.

Profile C can train:

> **Make the reasoning visible.**

---

## Decision-Making: Bring Values, Facts, and Boundaries Together

High-quality decisions can ask all of the following:

### Values
What matters to me?

### Facts
What is actually true?

### People
Who is affected?

### Boundary
What is unacceptable?

### Feasibility
What can realistically be done?

This is more reliable than only asking what “feels right.”

---

## Closure: Turn Long-Term Meaning into the Next Action

INFJ-related preferences may make long-term direction attractive.

Progress still requires:

> **Meaning → Next Action**

For example:

> “I want meaningful work.”

needs to become:

- What kinds of problems?
- What kinds of environments?
- What skills?
- What should I test next?
