# 01｜Core Personality Profile

## Move from Discovering Opportunities to Creating Value Consistently

Profile C shows a relatively clear ENFP preference structure.

You may often:

- obtain new information quickly through interaction;
- notice new opportunities, relationships, and directions;
- consider values, human impact, and authenticity in judgment;
- want room to adjust;
- enjoy connecting people, resources, and ideas;
- remain highly interested in growth and change.

The most important boundary is:

> **Clear preferences do not mean that creativity, social ability, leadership, or opportunity judgment is already mature.**

Capability requires real-world evidence.

---

## Move from “Many Opportunities” to Opportunity Selection

Once opportunity discovery becomes natural, the scarce resource is no longer more opportunity.

It is:

> **Choosing which opportunity deserves investment.**

Profile C can build screening criteria such as:

- Does it solve a real problem?
- Does it fit long-term values?
- Is there real demand?
- Are resources available to enter?
- Are you willing to continue?
- Is it worth giving up other opportunities?
- Can it create long-term accumulation?

This turns:

> **Opportunity Generation**

into:

> **Opportunity Selection**

---

## Move from Connecting People to Building Long-Term Relationship Capital

ENFP-related preferences may make building new connections feel natural.

Long-term value usually comes from:

- trust;
- consistent follow-through;
- clear boundaries;
- valuable information exchange;
- ability to handle disagreement;
- continuing beyond the novelty phase.

The Profile C task is:

> **Move from connection quantity to relationship quality.**

---

## Move from Open Adjustment to Completion and Compounding

Clear Perceiving may make adjustment and new options feel natural.

Long-term capability still requires:

- selection;
- completion;
- review;
- repetition;
- depth.

Use:

> **Explore → Select → Commit → Deliver → Review**

# 02｜Thinking & Decision Structure

## Information Processing: Move from Opportunity Sense to Evidence Quality

Clear Intuition may make it natural to notice:

- new markets;
- new relationships;
- new combinations;
- new possibilities;
- new directions.

Profile C should train:

> **Which opportunities deserve confidence?**

Ask:

1. What evidence exists?
2. Is this only emotional momentum?
3. Who genuinely needs it?
4. What are the competition and costs?
5. Can it be tested with a small experiment first?

---

## External Interaction: Move from Immediate Stimulation to Structured Judgment

Clear Extraversion may make high-interaction environments feel natural.

Profile C can use:

### Capture
Record important information.

### Distill
Extract the key point.

### Compare
Compare it with the main direction.

### Decide
Decide whether adjustment is warranted.

Without this structure, external stimulation can keep changing attention.

---

## Decision-Making: Values Are Not Enough Without Opportunity Cost

Clear Feeling may make it natural to ask:

- Do I like this?
- Is it meaningful?
- Does it help people?
- Does it fit my values?

A direction can be meaningful and still not be worth doing now.

Ask:

> **If I do this, what am I choosing not to do?**

Opportunity cost is an important Profile C decision variable.

---

## Closure: Build a Core Layer and an Experiment Layer

Organize development into:

### Core
Long-term main direction.

### Experiment
A small number of active tests.

### Network / Curiosity
Maintain awareness or connection without active investment.

This preserves openness while protecting compounding.
