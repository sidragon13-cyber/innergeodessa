# InnerGeo Professional Assessment Standard V1.0

**Status:** Draft Professional Standard
**Scope:** Personality / Career / Zodiac / Kids Interest Discovery
**Purpose:** Define the minimum professional, methodological, ethical, interpretive, and commercial quality standards for every InnerGeo assessment product.

---

## 1. Core Philosophy

InnerGeo does not decide a user's future.

InnerGeo helps users understand their interests, preferences, patterns, strengths, possible risks, and areas worth further exploration so they can make better-informed choices.

Every InnerGeo assessment must follow four principles:

1. Evidence before interpretation.
2. Preference is not ability.
3. Assessment is not destiny.
4. Every direction should remain open to real-world validation.

The system must never imply that a single test can determine a person's future, intelligence, worth, diagnosis, professional competence, or guaranteed life outcome.

---

## 2. Internal Quality Score vs User Results

The 100-point Professional Audit score is an internal product-quality metric only.

It evaluates the quality of the assessment itself.

It must never be shown to users as a score of the person.

### Internal Professional Audit

Each module is reviewed across ten dimensions:

1. Construct and Method
2. Item or Input Quality
3. Scoring or Calculation
4. Differentiation
5. Interpretation Quality
6. Report Depth
7. Actionability
8. Fairness and Bias
9. Boundaries and Ethics
10. Paid Value

Each dimension is scored from 0 to 10.

Total possible internal score: 100.

### User-Facing Results

User results must describe:

- interests
- preferences
- relative patterns
- stronger and weaker signals
- combinations
- possible development areas
- work or activity environments
- reflection points
- practical next steps

User results must not be framed as pass/fail, superior/inferior, gifted/not gifted, or successful/unsuccessful.

---

## 3. Construct Definition

Before an assessment is released, its target construct must be explicitly defined.

Examples:

### Personality

Measures preference patterns across defined personality dimensions.

It does not measure intelligence, mental health, clinical diagnosis, professional competence, or life success.

### Career Interest

Measures relative interest across defined activity and work-interest domains.

It does not directly measure ability, employability, job performance, or guaranteed career fit.

### Zodiac

Provides structured symbolic interpretation based on calculated birth-chart data.

It is not a scientific psychological assessment and must not be presented as medical, psychological, employment, financial, legal, or educational advice.

### Kids Interest Discovery

Measures current patterns of attraction, curiosity, participation preference, and willingness to explore different activity domains.

It does not measure fixed talent, intelligence, future occupation, or permanent ability.

---

## 4. Evidence Chain

Every important report conclusion should follow an identifiable evidence chain:

Input
↓
Scoring or Calculation
↓
Pattern
↓
Interpretation
↓
Practical Meaning
↓
Suggested Exploration or Action

Reports should avoid unsupported jumps from raw answers directly to major life recommendations.

---

## 5. Item and Input Quality

Assessment items must be reviewed for:

- construct relevance
- clarity
- age appropriateness
- ambiguity
- cultural bias
- gender stereotyping
- socioeconomic bias
- social desirability bias
- excessive context dependence
- duplicate meaning
- translation equivalence
- response-scale appropriateness

Every item must have a clear reason for being included.

Question-bank changes must preserve versioning and auditability.

Structural changes require a new question-bank version.

---

## 6. Scoring and Calculation

All scoring or calculation logic must be:

- deterministic where appropriate
- versioned
- testable
- reproducible
- documented
- independently validated where feasible

The system must distinguish between:

- strong signal
- moderate signal
- weak or emerging signal
- near-tie
- uncertain or mixed profile

A near-tie must not be interpreted with the same certainty as a large difference.

---

## 7. Interpretation Standard

Reports must describe tendencies rather than fixed limitations.

Preferred wording includes:

- tends to
- may prefer
- often finds
- may be more comfortable with
- can become more noticeable when
- worth exploring
- one possible interpretation is

Avoid:

- you will
- you cannot
- you are destined to
- you must become
- this proves
- this guarantees
- you are naturally bad at
- your child will become

Interpretations must include both strengths and trade-offs where relevant.

---

## 8. Differentiation Standard

Premium reports must provide meaningful individual differentiation.

A report is not considered sufficiently individualized if it only:

- replaces the result title
- swaps a small number of adjectives
- repeats generic motivational advice
- uses the same paragraphs across many result types
- ignores score strength or closeness

Individualization should consider, where available:

- dimension strength
- ranking
- score gaps
- combinations
- conflicting signals
- dynamic rules
- user-specific inputs
- age group
- relevant contextual factors

---

## 9. Report Depth

A Premium Report must provide clearly greater value than the free result.

Premium content should include:

- deeper explanation
- evidence behind the interpretation
- combination analysis
- strengths and trade-offs
- possible risks
- environmental fit
- practical reflection
- specific next actions

Additional length alone does not constitute additional value.

---

## 10. Actionability

Every Premium Report should answer:

1. What does this result mean?
2. Why might this pattern matter?
3. Where might it appear in real life?
4. What should the user observe?
5. What could the user try next?
6. How can the user validate whether the interpretation fits?

Recommendations should encourage experimentation rather than irreversible decisions.

---

## 11. Fairness and Bias

Assessment design must avoid measuring access or privilege when the intended construct is interest or preference.

Example:

Poor:
"Do you enjoy playing LEGO?"

Better:
"Would you enjoy building something from many small pieces?"

Poor:
"Do you like visiting science museums?"

Better:
"Would you like exploring how a machine or experiment works?"

The system should avoid unnecessary dependence on:

- expensive extracurricular exposure
- specific brands
- elite educational experiences
- specific cultural assumptions
- gendered occupational stereotypes

---

## 12. Bilingual Equivalence

English and Chinese versions must preserve the same construct meaning.

Translation must not:

- change scoring direction
- increase or reduce intensity
- introduce cultural assumptions
- add psychological claims
- simplify away important distinctions

Bilingual changes must be reviewed as assessment changes, not ordinary copy editing.

---

## 13. Boundaries and Ethics

InnerGeo products must not be presented as:

- medical diagnosis
- psychiatric diagnosis
- psychological diagnosis
- legal advice
- financial advice
- employment screening
- recruitment selection
- educational admission decisions
- credit decisions
- guaranteed career recommendations

Users must retain decision-making autonomy.

Assessments are tools for exploration and reflection.

---

## 14. Kids-Specific Standard

Kids Interest Discovery requires additional safeguards.

### Age Bands

6–8:
- image-first
- short instructions
- simple scenarios
- low reading burden
- parent-assisted comprehension

9–12:
- image plus short text
- situational items
- interest-strength items
- persistence items
- broader self-report capacity

### Parent Role

Parents may help explain questions.

Parents should not choose answers for the child.

Required guidance:

"Please help your child understand the question, but allow them to choose their own answer."

### Child-Safe Interpretation

Never describe a child as:

- weak
- bad at
- not talented
- unsuitable
- incapable
- destined for a profession

Use:

- currently drawn to
- enjoys exploring
- shows interest in
- worth trying
- emerging interest
- less frequently selected at this stage

### Data Minimization

Collect only what is necessary.

Preferred first-version child profile:

- nickname
- age band
- language
- assessment result
- assessment date

Avoid collecting unless clearly necessary:

- exact home address
- school name
- child phone number
- child photo
- unnecessary precise identifiers

## 15. Module-Specific Professionalism

### Personality

Professionalism depends on:

- quality of dimension measurement
- scoring transparency
- score-strength interpretation
- clear distinction between types
- avoidance of stereotypes
- meaningful dynamic personalization

### Career

Professionalism depends on:

- RIASEC construct coverage
- item quality
- dimension balance
- score-gap interpretation
- occupational mapping quality
- distinction between interest and ability
- actionable career exploration

### Zodiac

Professionalism depends on:

- accurate astronomical inputs
- correct time-zone handling
- reproducible calculations
- consistent interpretation rules
- clear symbolic framing
- explicit non-scientific boundary

### Kids

Professionalism depends on:

- developmental appropriateness
- visual neutrality
- low reading burden
- fairness
- parent guidance
- interest rather than talent labeling
- practical exploration recommendations

## 16. Professional Audit Scale

Internal release interpretation:

- 90–100: Professional Release
- 80–89: Commercially Ready with defined improvement items
- 70–79: Structurally complete but professional weaknesses remain
- Below 70: Major revision required

A high engineering-validation score cannot substitute for weak construct or content quality.

A validator PASS means the implementation is structurally correct.

It does not by itself prove psychological validity.

---

## 17. Validation Levels

InnerGeo distinguishes three validation levels:

### Level 1 — Engineering Validated

Confirms:

- structure
- data contracts
- scoring implementation
- calculation implementation
- frontend rendering
- storage
- version compatibility

### Level 2 — Professionally Reviewed

Confirms:

- construct clarity
- item quality
- interpretation quality
- report depth
- fairness
- boundaries
- actionability

### Level 3 — Psychometrically Validated

Requires real user data and statistical evidence such as:

- reliability
- internal consistency
- test-retest stability
- construct validity
- criterion relationships where appropriate
- subgroup fairness
- item performance

InnerGeo must not claim Level 3 unless supporting evidence exists.

---

## 18. Change Priority

Audit findings must be classified as:

### P0 — Critical

Could make the assessment misleading, unsafe, structurally wrong, or professionally indefensible.

### P1 — High

Meaningfully reduces trust, interpretation accuracy, or paid value.

### P2 — Medium

Improves clarity, personalization, or usability but does not undermine the core assessment.

### P3 — Low

Cosmetic, stylistic, or optional enhancement.

Only P0 and P1 findings should automatically trigger near-term product changes.

Do not rewrite entire reports because of isolated P2 or P3 issues.

## 20. InnerGeo Professional Principle

InnerGeo does not label people.

InnerGeo helps people notice patterns.

InnerGeo does not decide the future.

InnerGeo helps users make better-informed choices.

InnerGeo does not promise certainty.

InnerGeo provides structured evidence, interpretation, and directions worth exploring.
