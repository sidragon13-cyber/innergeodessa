## 3. Core Measurement Boundary

The illustration must:

> illustrate the activity

and must not:

> require the child to perform the activity.

This is especially important for:

- pattern reasoning
- sequencing
- logical integration
- observation
- construction

The target construct remains:

**Interest in exploring the activity.**

It is not:

**Ability to complete the activity.**
