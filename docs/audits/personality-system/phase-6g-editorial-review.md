# Phase 6G — Final Editorial Review

Date: 2026-07-29  
Starting baseline: `8201b58 test(reports): complete Phase 6F sixteen-type audit`

## Review scope and method

All 16 personality reports, 288 canonical sections, 1,120 base content blocks, and 816 dynamic rules were reviewed in four comparison batches. The review used an evidence-driven threshold: grammar damage, mechanical substitution, semantic collision, generic action guidance, base/rule inconsistency, or safety risk. Subjective polishing was left unchanged or recorded in the backlog.

The batches were:

- INTJ, INTP, INFJ, INFP
- ENTJ, ESTJ, ENFJ, ESFJ
- ENTP, ENFP, ESTP, ESFP
- ISTJ, ISFJ, ISTP, ISFP

## Corrections made

### Introverted conceptual and values-oriented types

- **INTJ:** reviewed; no concrete correction required.
- **INTP:** differentiated collaboration, motivation, and the full 90-day plan around question framing, competing models, tests, explanation, and bounded closure.
- **INFJ:** differentiated motivation, collaboration, and action planning around purpose, consent, agency, invited correction, and transfer of development ownership.
- **INFP:** replaced an INTP-adjacent decision block and inherited action plan with value trade-offs, authentic expression, observable impact, and explicit commitment.

### Extraverted leadership and coordination types

- **ENTJ:** reviewed; no concrete correction required.
- **ESTJ:** rewrote motivation, change risk/guidance, and the 90-day plan around operating standards, frontline evidence, handoffs, exceptions, and sustainable procedure.
- **ENFJ:** corrected damaged control language and separated support from overreach through consent, recipient-defined development, and independent agency.
- **ESFJ:** corrected malformed phrases and replaced strategic-development inheritance with present practical needs, shared care, visible ownership, safe opt-out, and aftercare.

### Extraverted adaptive and exploratory types

- **ENTP:** reviewed; no concrete correction required.
- **ENFP:** separated values-led possibility activation from ENTP debate through freely chosen participation, consent, and meaningful follow-through.
- **ESTP:** corrected a damaged title and replaced an ESTJ-derived plan with live observation, bounded tactical trials, debriefs, and transferable response capability.
- **ESFP:** replaced abstract possibility and intellectual-catalyst inheritance with direct experience, sensory and social evidence, participation choice, access, and responsibility after the event.

### Introverted stabilizing and responsive types

- **ISTJ:** replaced strategic-template language with accumulated evidence, standards, procedures, exception review, and dependable shared ownership.
- **ISFJ:** reviewed; no concrete correction required.
- **ISTP:** replaced strategic/procedural inheritance with mechanism diagnosis, direct testing, safe adaptive response, reproducibility, and maintainable handover.
- **ISFP:** replaced inherited analytical action planning with values-grounded craft, sensory/practical choices, lived impact, and visible completion.

## Dynamic-rule findings

The review corrected ESFJ confidence, dimension, and combination wording; ISTJ and ISTP dimension/combination wording; ISTP low-confidence guidance; and one defective ISTJ rule ID (`prodirectional` → `provisional`). Conditions, priorities, targets, slots, inventory counts, and registry behavior were preserved.

No rule contradicts its audited base positioning. Exact normalized cross-type rule-content duplication is zero. High lexical similarity remains in shared confidence bands and adjacent-preference qualifiers; this is acceptable where the conditional meaning and personality-specific guidance remain correct.

## Action-plan findings

The most important issue was mechanically inherited 90-day planning. Controlled rewrites now give:

- INTP: frame, compare, test, explain, decide, transfer.
- INFJ: check meaning, obtain consent, invite correction, share ownership.
- INFP: choose a value, express it, observe impact, commit.
- ESTJ: map the operation, pilot procedure, standardize supported practice.
- ENFJ: audit consent, build agency, transfer development ownership.
- ESFJ: expose invisible care, share coordination, sustain community support.
- ESTP: observe the live system, run short action cycles, transfer response capability.
- ESFP: observe participation, share activity ownership, integrate aftercare.
- ISTJ: review evidence, revise procedure, stabilize shared ownership.
- ISTP: isolate mechanism, test diagnosis, make the fix transferable.
- ISFP: make a first form, refine through feedback, complete and integrate.

Existing INTJ, ENTJ, ENTP, and ISFJ plans were retained because they were already concrete and aligned.

## Safety and methodology

The content remains reflective and educational. It does not claim diagnosis, treatment, fixed destiny, scientifically certain individual prediction, or type-determined employment, educational, financial, relationship, or medical outcomes. Corrections strengthened consent, user agency, opt-out, evidence, boundaries, and reversible testing.

This review is not clinical or psychometric validation. Wording concerning stress, relationships, and development still warrants independent domain review before high-stakes use.

## Remaining limitations

- Automated similarity detects shared vocabulary, not conceptual equivalence or subtle contradiction.
- Smoke profiles are representative, not exhaustive proof of every rule-condition combination.
- Editorial classification reflects this repository review, not independent clinical, psychological, or psychometric assessment.
- Shared V1 version constants do not express per-personality editorial revision history.
- Browser, mobile, and print acceptance remain separate release checks even for editorially ready types.
