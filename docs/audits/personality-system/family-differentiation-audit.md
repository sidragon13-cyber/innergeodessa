## Explorers

Members: ISTP, ISFP, ESTP, ESFP. Membership and structural validation pass. Positioning separates hands-on diagnosis, values-sensitive craft, tactical engagement, and participative experience.

Concrete malformed wording in ISTP, ESTP, and ESFP was corrected. This is the most recently implemented family and should receive the highest-priority human editorial pass, especially where automated substitutions produced abstract or unnatural phrasing.

Phase 6G made the largest controlled corrections in this family: ISTP now centres practical diagnosis and transferable fixes, ESTP centres live tactical response, ESFP centres immediate shared experience and aftercare, and ISFP centres values-grounded craft. All pass structural and generator validation, but ISTP, ESTP, and ESFP still require an independent whole-report read before frontend release.
