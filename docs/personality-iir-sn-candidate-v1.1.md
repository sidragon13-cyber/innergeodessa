> This document reviews all 36 SN candidates together. Scores are structured expert judgments for wording and construct control, not empirical psychometric statistics.

## 1. Review framework

Each item was scored from 1 to 5 on construct fit, clarity, neutrality, accessibility and distinctiveness. The total possible score is 25. Decisions combine these scores with duplicate risk and construct-contamination review; they are not based on score totals alone.

| Criterion | Review question |
| --- | --- |
| Construct fit | Does the item primarily measure the intended SN information preference? |
| Clarity | Can the item be understood quickly and consistently? |
| Neutrality | Does it avoid presenting either pole as smarter, more creative, safer or more practical? |
| Accessibility | Does it avoid unnecessary vocabulary, education and cultural demands? |
| Distinctiveness | Does it add information beyond nearby candidates? |
