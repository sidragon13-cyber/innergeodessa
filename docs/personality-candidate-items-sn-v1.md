## 1. Purpose

This document creates the first SN candidate-item pool. It provides six alternatives for each of six subdimensions so weak, contaminated, inaccessible or overlapping items can later be revised or removed without narrowing construct coverage.

## 2. SN construct boundaries

SN describes relative preferences in how people attend to, organise and explore information: concrete details or broader meanings; directly checkable evidence or tentative inference; present conditions or future possibilities; practical use or conceptual exploration; separate facts or cross-context patterns; and established methods or alternative approaches.

It must not primarily measure intelligence, education, memory, creativity, imagination, artistic talent, technical skill, attention disorder, sensory acuity, risk-taking, optimism, open-mindedness, conscientiousness or resistance to change. Sensing and Intuition are equally legitimate information preferences, and both remain available regardless of the leading result.

## 3. Required subdimensions

1. Concrete detail focus.
2. Evidence and inference preference.
3. Present reality and future possibility.
4. Practical application and conceptual exploration.
5. Pattern and connection seeking.
6. Established methods and alternative approaches.

## 4. Item balance rules

The 36 candidates use a five-point agreement scale. The pool contains 18 S-keyed and 18 N-keyed items, with three items for each pole in every subdimension. Wording uses plain international English, one main proposition, no double negatives, no direct type labels and no assumptions about employment, university, family, wealth or lifestyle.

`Ascending` means agreement contributes toward the recorded keyed preference. `Direct` and `counter-keyed` describe wording strategy, not grammatical positivity and not reverse scoring.

## 5. Metadata conventions

Each row records a stable candidate ID, family and blueprint trace, subdimension, keyed pole, scoring direction, wording type, item form, context, candidate wording, social-desirability risk, construct-contamination risk, accessibility risk, main confound and reviewer note. Risk ratings are `L`, `M` and `H` and are review prompts rather than empirical findings.

## 8. Construct-contamination audit

| Risk area | Main affected subdimensions | Control used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intelligence or education | Detail, application, connection | Items ask about preference or sequence, never accuracy or ability. |
| Creativity or imagination | Future possibility, alternative approaches | Items avoid praise words such as original, innovative or visionary. |
| Conscientiousness / JP | Present reality, established methods | Items target information focus or starting method, not planning, order or completion. |
| Risk tolerance | Evidence and inference, alternative approaches | Items use tentative and low-stakes wording and do not reward unsafe action. |
| Attention or sensory differences | Concrete detail focus | Items avoid claims of superior perception and retain preference framing. |
| Optimism, anxiety or rumination | Future possibility | Items ask about possible development without positive or negative emotional direction. |
| Academic prestige | Conceptual exploration, pattern connection | Contexts include everyday information and practical tasks, not only formal study. |
